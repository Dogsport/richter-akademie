Fault Identified

Crooked Sit

Reasoning & Analysis

Upon the decoy’s standstill, the dog fails to maintain a shoulder-parallel alignment with the handler. The rear haunches are canted 15 degrees to the left, indicating a lack of focus on the decoy and a physical anticipation of the handler's next command. Per the IGP regulations, this must be penalized in the 'Technical' score component.
